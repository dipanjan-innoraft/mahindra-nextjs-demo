FAQS

Visit the official Careers at Mahindra portal to explore current Mahindra job vacancy listings. All applications are submitted online through the formal Mahindra recruitment process.

All open positions across Mahindra Group companies are accessible to SOAR participants. You can explore and apply for these roles directly through the Mahindra Group Careers website or the individual company career pages listed on the SOAR portal.

Admissions are managed directly by Mahindra University. Visit the university’s official website, review the eligibility criteria for your chosen programme and submit the online application form with the required documents before the deadline. For entrance requirements and timelines, please refer to the university’s admissions section.

Qualifications vary depending on the role and business unit. Each Mahindra job vacancy clearly outlines the required educational background, experience and skill set. Opportunities are available across functions and sectors, including Tech Mahindra careers, Mahindra Logistics careers and Club Mahindra careers, ranging from early-career roles to specialised leadership positions.

A job in Mahindra company offers structured growth, early responsibility and exposure to businesses that operate at scale. We place strong emphasis on ethical conduct, inclusive practices and long-term professional development, creating a workplace where performance and purpose go hand in hand.

Beware of Fraudulent Recruiting

It has been brought to our notice that certain people are misleading job applicants by soliciting allegedly refundable security deposits monies towards registration / interview fees etc. for job opportunities at Mahindra Group Companies. The said persons are also using our trademarks and other information relating to our executives.

Please be informed that we have not published or issued any such communications, nor have we authorized anyone else to do so. We do not and will not solicit monies from job applicants for any reason whatsoever.

We are not responsible for any claims or actions arising out of dealings, monetary or otherwise, with the aforesaid persons. We, however, reserve our rights to initiate any proceedings or legal actions against such persons, as we may deem fit.
