Our Story

1945 - Present

HomeAbout UsOur Story

Key Moments

1945 - 1961

1945

  • M&M is founded

    Two brothers, JC and KC Mahindra along with Ghulam Mohammed set up 'Mahindra & Mohammed' on the 2nd of October, 1945.

  • M&M begins steel trading

1949

  • The Jeep hits Indian shores

    75 Jeeps come in from Willys Overland Export Corporation, USA in CKD condition, and are assembled at Mazgaon, Bombay.

  • M&M and Mitsubishi strike an Iron and Steel deal

    5,000 tons of steel sourced to build wagons.

1956

  • M&M goes public

    The company announces going public on June 15, 1955, and lists its shares on the Bombay Stock Exchange in 1956.

1961

  • M&M ties up with International Harvester, USA

    Mahindra joins hands with the US manufacturer of agricultural machinery, construction equipment, trucks, automobiles, and household and commercial products, for the manufacture of tractors.

1962 - 1985

1962

  • Mahindra Ugine Steel Company formed

    M&M and Ugine Kuhlmann, France, come together to form MUSCO, Mahindra Ugine Steel Company, manufacturing alloy. Pandit Jawahar Lal Nehru visits the factory.

1963

  • Keshub Mahindra appointed Chairman

    After the demise of the company's co-founder, KC Mahindra, Mr. Keshub Mahindra becomes the second Chairman of M&M Ltd.

  • ITCI is formed.

    The International Tractor Company of India (ITCI), a JV with International Harvester Company and Voltas Ltd. is formed with a license to manufacture 3,500 tractors.

1964

  • The Pope arrives in a Mahindra Jeep

    M&M specially manufactured the Jeep that drove His Holiness Pope Paul VI and Valerian Cardinal Gracias as they blessed thousands during the Eucharistic Congress in Bombay on the 3rd of December, 1964.

1965

  • UV and Tractor factories come up in Bombay

    Mahindra shuts down the factory in Mazgaon and establishes a plant in Kandivali to consolidate its operations for the manufacture of UVs and tractors.

1977

  • ITCI merges with M&M Ltd.

    The very successful International Tractor Company of India merges with M&M to become its tractor division.

1979

  • M&M ties up with Peugeot, France

    M&M receives a license from Automobiles Peugeot France to manufacture XDP 4.90 diesel engines. The assembly of Peugeot engines commences in Ghatkopar.

1983

  • M&M is a leader in the Indian Tractor Market

    Mahindra becomes the largest selling tractor brand in India, and holds the position for four decades now.

1986 - 1996

1986

  • Mahindra British Telecom formed

    Mahindra enters the telecom IT services space as Mahindra British Telecom, a joint venture with British Telecom, UK, which went on to become 'Tech Mahindra'.

1991

  • Mahindra Finance is incorporated

    Maxi Motors Financial Services Limited was rechristened as Mahindra & Mahindra Financial Services Limited with a fresh Certificate of Incorporation.

1993

  • Mahindra Steel Service Centre Ltd. is born

    Mahindra Steel Service Centre Limited is formed in association with Mitsubishi Corporation and Nisho Iwai Corporation of Japan.

1994

  • Strategic restructure of the Mahindra Group

    Mahindra Group's six strategic business units include Automotive, Farm Equipment, Financial Services, IT, Infrastructure Development Services and Steel Trading.

  • Mahindra USA is born

1996

  • Mahindra Holidays and Resorts, India Ltd is born

    MHRIL opens its first holiday resort in Munnar, Kerala.

  • Project Nanhi Kali is initiated

    Set up by the K. C. Mahindra Education Trust (KCMET), Nanhi Kali provides primary education to underprivileged girl children in India.

1997 - 2013

1997

  • Anand Mahindra becomes Managing Director

    Mr. R.K. Pitamber retires and hands over the reins to Anand Mahindra.

  • The Mahindra United World College of India is inaugurated

    One of 15 in the world, UWC is inaugurated by Queen Noor of Jordan in Pune, Maharashtra.

1999

  • MMFSL becomes a subsidiary of M&M Ltd

    Mahindra & Mahindra Financial Services Ltd., which began operations in 1995, becomes a subsidiary of M&M. The company plays a major role in financing Mahindra's UVs.

2000

  • M&M gets a makeover

    The millennium year brings in the unveiling of a new and visually dynamic group logo, signifying the road ahead.

  • The Mahindra Bolero is launched

    The Mahindra Bolero grows on to become India's largest selling SUV for ten years in a row.

2002

  • Mahindra World City - India's first integrated city is inaugurated

    Dr. J. Jayalalithaa, Chief Minister of Tamil Nadu inaugurates 'Mahindra World City' in Chennai. The project is promoted by the Mahindra Group, Infrastructure Leasing & Financial Services Ltd. and Tamil Nadu Industrial Development Corporation Ltd.

  • The Mahindra Scorpio is launched

2004

  • Mahindra Tractors wins the prestigious Deming Prize
  • The Mahindra Group acquires a majority stake in Bristlecone Inc.
  • Mahindra Tractors crosses a major milestone

2007

  • Employability with Empathy and Emotion

    Mahindra Pride Schools begin empowering young people.

2008

  • The Aftermarket sector is formed
  • Automotive facility opens in Chakan - Pune

2010

  • Mahindra sponsors the FIFA World Cup
  • Mahindra acquires Reva Electric Vehicles
  • Mahindra Blues, India's biggest Blues music festival is launched.

2011

  • Mahindra Rise is launched

2012

  • Mahindra Research Valley in Chennai is inaugurated

2013

  • Tech Mahindra and Mahindra Satyam merge

2014 - 2025

2014

  • Mahindra enters FIA Formula E Championships

2016

  • Mahindra acquires Sampo Rosenlew

2018

  • The iconic Jawa returns to India

    Mahindra's Classic Legends Pvt. Ltd. brings authentic nostalgia to the motorcycle market with the revival of legendary brands like the Jawa, Yezdi and BSA.

2019

  • Mahindra unveils the Pininfarina, the world's first and fastest Electric Hypercar

2020

  • Mahindra relaunches the Thar to rave reviews

    Redefining the timeless design, the all new Thar comes with reliable power and fresh interiors.

2021

  • Mahindra launches the XUV 700 Series

    A powerhouse of an SUV with sci-fi engineering, spirited performance and world-class safety.

2022

  • Mahindra launches the Scorpio-N

    Known as the Big Daddy of SUVs, the Scorpio-N combines advanced technology, superior comfort and intuitive safety features.

  • Our prestigious Padma Bhushan

    Anand Mahindra was awarded the Padma Bhushan by the president of India, Shri Ram Nath Kovind.

  • Mahindra unveils 5 new, future-ready electric SUVs designed by Born Electric

2023

  • Launch of Mahindra OJA: Transforming Farming in India (15 Aug 2023)

    Mahindra Tractors, the world's largest tractor manufacturer by volume, has officially launched – 4 Revolutionary OJA Tractor Platforms Globally; Launches 7 Revolutionary Lightweight 4WD Tractors – at Futurescape, an event held in Cape Town, South Africa.

2024

  • Thar ROXX Launch: A Milestone in Automotive Excellence (15 Aug 2024)

    With its iconic design and advanced M_GLYDE platform, offers an exceptionally smooth ride with crisp handling. It seamlessly blends Thar's outdoor DNA with modern sophistication, providing a premium SUV experience for those who demand the finest.

  • Mahindra Recognized in TIME's ‘World's Most Sustainable Companies 2024’

    Mahindra Group proudly secured a spot in TIME Magazine’s ‘World's Most Sustainable Companies 2024’ list, ranking 5th as the most sustainable automotive company. This accolade underscores Mahindra's unwavering commitment to sustainability and environmental stewardship.

2025

  • M&M Honored with ‘Company of the Year’ Award by ET (Jan 2025)

    Wins the prestigious ‘Company of the Year Award’ at the ET Corporate Excellence Awards, recognizing their outstanding achievements and leadership in the industry.

  • Launch of Mahindra BE 6 and XEV 9e Electric SUVs (14th Feb 2025)

    Unveils the BE 6 and XEV 9e, crafted on India’s revolutionary pure-electric architecture, INGLO. These innovative SUVs redefine performance, luxury, and sophistication in the electric mobility landscape.

  • SML Isuzu acquisition (1st Aug 2025)

    Completed its strategic acquisition of SML Isuzu, enhancing its position in the commercial vehicle sector. This move promises to combine their strengths, drive innovation, and expand product offerings both domestically and internationally.
